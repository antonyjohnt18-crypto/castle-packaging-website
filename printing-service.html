Printing Service

Flexographic and full-colour digital printing on your own bags, covers and flyers — no manufacturing required.

Two Ways To Print

Bring what you have. We print your brand on it.

Already have paper bags, covers, non-woven bags or flyers? Our Bengaluru facility prints your logo and artwork on them — choose the process that suits your design and order size.

Flexographic Printing

2-Colour · Your Own Bags, Covers or Flyers

Ideal for logos and simple one- or two-colour branding on larger runs. Bring your paper bags, covers, non-woven bags or flyers and we print your branding on them using 2-colour flexographic printing.

Priced per piece and per colour

Digital Printing

Full Colour · Single Pass

Ideal for full-colour artwork, photographs and short runs. Our single-pass digital press prints multi-colour designs in one pass on a wide range of paper packaging, with no plates or set-up costs for each design.

Priced per print — request a quote

Which Should I Choose?

Flexo or digital

Not sure which suits your job? Tell us your item, quantity and artwork and we'll recommend one.

Choose flexographic if…

Your design is a logo or simple one- or two-colour artwork, and you are printing larger quantities of the same design.

Choose digital if…

Your design uses many colours, gradients or photographs, or you need several different designs in smaller runs.

How It Works

From artwork to finished print
