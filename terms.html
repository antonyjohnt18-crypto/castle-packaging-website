Quotes and orders

A quote is an estimate of price and lead time until you confirm it. An order starts only when you confirm it and pay the advance described below. Please check your item, quantity, size, artwork and date carefully before you confirm, because we make and print what you confirm.

Payment

Payment is by UPI. Online payment through a website checkout is not available. Work does not start until a 50% advance payment is received. The balance is as confirmed in your quote.

Made-to-order and ready-stock items

Made-to-size paper bags and covers are manufactured to your specification, from 2000 bags or 300 kg, and take 7–15 days. They are not available as rush orders. Standard sizes are sold from ready stock, in packets (pack size is shown in the size chart for each size) or per kg, and are subject to availability. Sizes and paper types in stock may change from time to time.

Rush orders
