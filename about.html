About Castle Packaging

A paper packaging manufacturer with roots going back to 2013, now based in Bengaluru.

Our Story

How we started

Our journey in paper packaging began in 2013, when we entered the UAE market with a focus on manufacturing premium, customized paper bags. Between 2013 and 2017, we specialized in luxury paper bags for the Middle Eastern market, serving businesses across jewellery, perfumes, optics, chocolates, retail and other premium segments.

That period was built around high-end, fully customized paper bags, where every detail mattered — from paper thickness and structure to multi-colour printing, embossing, spot UV and other premium finishing techniques. It gave us hands-on expertise in customization, quality control and the exacting expectations of premium brands, which still shapes how we manufacture today.

Castle Packaging — Today

A full-range paper packaging manufacturer in Bengaluru

Today, Castle Packaging operates as a paper packaging manufacturing and servicing company based in Bengaluru, offering a wide range of paper-based products including paper bags, paper covers, tissue papers, paper cups, paper plates, and customized printed packaging. From ready-finished bags to bag-to-bag printing and mass production, we provide end-to-end solutions based on our customers' requirements.

Our strength lies in our ability to provide 360° support across the paper-bag manufacturing process — from customization and printing to production and finishing — helping customers manage their packaging requirements through a single, reliable partner. With our experience in the paper packaging industry and a strong focus on manufacturing and service, Castle Packaging is committed to delivering practical, scalable, customized and quality-focused packaging solutions.

What we manufacture

Paper bags in kraft, recycled and virgin material; paper covers; paper cups; and paper tissues across semi soft, soft and virgin cellulose fibre grades. Every product can be custom printed with your branding.

Who we serve

Retailers, food and beverage businesses, distributors and brands across Bengaluru and beyond who need paper packaging in bulk, with or without custom printing.

Our Mission & Vision

Why we do what we do

Our Mission

To manufacture reliable, high-quality paper packaging — bags, covers, cups and tissues — that gives businesses a genuinely sustainable, brand-ready alternative to plastic, backed by consistent supply and on-time delivery.

Our Vision

To be Bengaluru's most trusted name in paper packaging — known for quality, dependability, and a genuine commitment to sustainable manufacturing as demand for eco-friendly packaging keeps growing.
