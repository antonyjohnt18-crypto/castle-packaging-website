Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about paper bags, digital printing, minimum orders and rush orders.

What does Castle Packaging manufacture?

Castle Packaging manufactures paper bags (kraft, recycled and virgin), paper covers, paper cups and paper tissues, all made in-house in Bengaluru with custom printing available on every product.

Is Castle Packaging a manufacturer or a reseller?

Castle Packaging is a direct manufacturer, not a reseller. Every product is made in-house, which keeps lead times and pricing in our control.

Where is Castle Packaging based?

Castle Packaging is based at No. 11, Pipe Line Road, Balaji Layout, Kempapura, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560090, India.

What is a paper cover and who uses it?

A paper cover is a V-bottom kraft paper packaging item without handles, sold by weight (per kg). They're commonly used by bakeries, pharmacies and grocery or supermarket stores.

Can I get custom printing on my products?

Yes — every product Castle Packaging makes can be custom printed with your logo and branding. We also offer full-colour digital printing on finished items you supply, such as bags, boxes, bagasse, cloth and jute, with no minimum order. 2-colour flexographic printing is available on paper bags and covers for larger quantities.

What sizes are paper bags and covers available in?

Paper bags and covers are available in custom sizes. Please contact our representative for details on sizes, stock and minimum quantities.

How are paper cups and paper tissues sold?

Paper cups are sold per packet of 60 cups, in multiple sizes. Paper tissues are sold per packet of 70 pulls, across hard, semi soft, soft and virgin cellulose fibre grades.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum order for digital printing — even a single piece can be printed. For paper bag and cover manufacturing, please contact our representative for details.

Do you take rush or last-minute orders?

Yes. Small digital print jobs can be ready within minutes once your artwork is approved. For larger quantities, tell us your needed-by date and we confirm what we can deliver.

I make jute, cloth or paper bags. Can you print them?

Yes. Send us your finished bags and we print your customers’ designs in full colour — many designs, small runs, no minimum. See Printing for Bag Makers.

What do you digitally print on?

Finished items in paper and board, bagasse, cloth and jute: paper bags, pizza and burger boxes, T-shirts, pouches, tote bags, plates, menus, tags and more. See Digital Printing.
