Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our paper bags, covers, cups, tissues, custom printing and ordering.

What does Castle Packaging manufacture?

Castle Packaging manufactures paper bags (kraft, recycled and virgin), paper covers, paper cups and paper tissues, all made in-house in Bengaluru with custom printing available on every product.

Is Castle Packaging a manufacturer or a reseller?

Castle Packaging is a direct manufacturer, not a reseller. Every product is made in-house, which keeps lead times and pricing in our control.

Where is Castle Packaging based?

Castle Packaging is based at No. 11, Pipe Line Road, Balaji Layout, Kempapura, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560090, India.

What is a paper cover and who uses it?

A paper cover is a V-bottom kraft paper packaging item without handles, sold by weight (per kg). They're commonly used by bakeries, pharmacies and grocery or supermarket stores.

Can I get custom printing on my products?

Yes — every product Castle Packaging makes can be custom printed with your logo and branding. If you already have paper bags, covers, non-woven bags or flyers, Castle Packaging also offers a standalone 2-colour flexographic printing service on items you supply.

What sizes are paper bags and covers available in?

Paper bags are available in custom sizes from W10×H16cm up to W42×H60cm. Paper covers are available in a range of sizes and sold by weight.

How are paper cups and paper tissues sold?

Paper cups are sold per packet of 60 cups, in multiple sizes. Paper tissues are sold per packet of 70 pulls, across hard, semi soft, soft and virgin cellulose fibre grades.
